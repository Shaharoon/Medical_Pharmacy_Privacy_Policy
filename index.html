1. Introduction

Welcome to Medical Pharmacy ("we", "our", or "us"), owned and operated by Haider's Developers. Medical Pharmacy is a digital ledger and pharmacy management application designed to help pharmacy owners, medical store managers, and healthcare retailers securely manage medicine inventory, customer balance records, print POS receipts, and keep financial data synchronized.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, how it is kept secure, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

A. Merchant Profile Information

When you create or update an account for your pharmacy, we may collect:

  • Store / Pharmacy Name and Full Name
  • Contact Phone / WhatsApp Number
  • Email Address
  • City / Location
  • Encrypted credentials (or basic Google profile information if signing in via Google Sign-In)

B. Customer & Inventory Transaction Ledger Data

To provide core pharmacy billing and bookkeeping services, you enter store operational data into the app, which may include:

  • Customer and Supplier Names and Phone Numbers (for balance tracking and bill reminders)
  • Credit (Udhar) and Cash/Debit transaction amounts, transaction dates, and bill notes
  • Medicine stock details, bill/prescription images, or audio notes attached to ledger entries

C. Biometric Authentication Data (Fingerprint)

  • If you enable fingerprint or biometric login, the verification is processed 100% locally on your device by the Android OS BiometricPrompt API.
  • We do NOT collect, transmit, access, or store your biometric data on any server.

D. Device and Technical Information

  • Device manufacturer, OS version, network status, app crash logs, and Android Advertising ID (AAID) used for app stability and advertising.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your merchant account and manage multi-device login sessions.
  • Synchronize, backup, and restore your digital medical store records securely in the cloud.
  • Allow you to send bill summaries and payment reminders to your customers via third-party messaging apps (such as WhatsApp) upon your direct action.
  • Deliver non-personalized or personalized advertisements via Google Mobile Ads.
  • Diagnose crashes, maintain application security, and prevent fraud.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers to support our infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC):
    • Firebase Authentication: For secure account sign-in.
    • Cloud Firestore: For encrypted cloud storage and multi-device data sync.
    • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob (Google LLC):
    • To display advertisements within the application.
    • Google Ads Privacy & Terms

5. Data Security

All communications between the Medical Pharmacy mobile application and our servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). User passwords and credentials are cryptographically protected.

6. Data Retention and Account / Data Deletion

  • Retention: We retain your account and store ledger data as long as your merchant account remains active to provide continuous cloud backup.
  • Data Deletion: You have the full right to delete your account and all associated records at any time.
    • To request complete account and data deletion, send an email to shaharoonhaider@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Medical Pharmacy", mentioning your registered email or phone number.
    • Upon verification, your account and all associated cloud records will be permanently removed within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Medical Pharmacy is intended for business owners and merchants aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
